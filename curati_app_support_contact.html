Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about Curati App.

How can boutique hotels and spas use Curati App?

Hotels and spas can purchase commercial B2B subscriptions to play our 432 Hz relaxing audio streams in lobbies, treatment rooms, or provide guest room access codes for in-room relaxation.

What languages are available in the app?

Curati App is fully localized in 5 global languages: English, Español, Français, Português, and Nederlands. You can switch languages easily inside the app settings.

Can I mix frequencies and create my own custom playlists?

Yes! Curati App allows users to select pre-made 432 Hz ambient music or mix individual relaxing frequencies to build, save, and loop custom playlists for meditation, massage, or focus.

Who created the audio and frequencies in Curati App?

All audio tracks, ambient soundscapes, and 432 Hz frequencies are engineered using cutting-edge Artificial Intelligence by creator Dj Ronny Woods, available exclusively inside Curati App.
