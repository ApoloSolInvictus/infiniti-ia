Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Curati App and the English Solfeggio synthesizer at infiniti-ia.com/english handle information. The app is operated under the Infiniti IA / W Studio name.

Information the app does not request

The app does not require an account and does not intentionally request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, photos, files, health information, or microphone access. The synthesizer creates tones in the device's web view; it does not record, upload, or analyze microphone audio.

Information processed to deliver the service

When the app loads the remote synthesizer, the website host, content delivery networks, and other infrastructure providers may receive ordinary technical request information such as an IP address, device or browser user agent, requested URL, date and time, approximate region, and error or security logs. This information is used to deliver, protect, troubleshoot, and maintain the website. Infiniti IA does not use the synthesizer to build user profiles, sell personal information, or serve behavioral advertising.

Web resources and third parties

The English synthesizer loads web resources from the Infiniti IA website and external providers, including Vercel hosting, Google Fonts, unpkg, Tailwind CSS, React, ReactDOM, Babel, and Phosphor Icons. Those providers may process technical request data under their own privacy policies. The app does not add native analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking SDKs.

The English synthesizer does not submit prompts, images, or audio to the OpenAI endpoints used by other Infiniti IA pages. Those separate website features are outside the app flow described by this policy.

One-time Google Play purchase

Curati App offers a non-consumable one-time purchase identified as Healing Frequencies Full Access. The price shown by Google Play may be US$7.77 depending on country, currency, and applicable taxes. There is no free trial, subscription, automatic renewal, or later charge. Google Play processes the payment and payment-method details; the app does not receive or store card numbers. The app receives purchase status and a technical transaction identifier from Google Play to confirm permanent access and restore the purchase.

Cookies and local storage

The app does not intentionally set advertising cookies. The embedded web view and its providers may use ordinary cache, cookie, or local-storage mechanisms needed for website delivery and security. You can remove the app and its local web data by deleting the app from your Android device or iPhone.

Data retention and security

Infiniti IA does not maintain a user account or personal profile for this app. Technical logs may be retained by the hosting and infrastructure providers for security, reliability, and legal purposes according to their policies. We use HTTPS for the app's connection to the website, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not use the sound experience at an uncomfortable volume.

Your choices

You can stop using the app, remove it from your device, disable network access through your device settings, or contact Infiniti IA through infiniti-ia.com with questions about this policy. Because the app does not create user accounts, there is no account deletion workflow.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app, website, or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a new version is published.
