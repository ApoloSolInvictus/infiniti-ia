Privacy Policy & App Info

LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026
APPLICABLE FOR: GLOBAL USERS (EN, ES, FR, PT, NL)

Welcome to the Curati App. Your privacy and wellness are our top priorities. This document outlines how we handle your data, our service terms, and provides important information regarding the intellectual property of the content you experience within our platform.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a seamless experience across our global user base, we collect minimal operational data. This includes:

  • Language Preferences: To serve the app in English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, or Dutch based on your selection.
  • Usage Data: Anonymous metrics on which frequencies and playlists are used most in boutique hotels, spas, and personal meditation sessions to improve our offerings.
  • Account Data: If you create an account to save custom playlists, we store your basic login credentials securely.

Audio Architecture & Authorship

The core of the Curati App experience is our exclusive library of 432 Hz relaxing frequencies and music. We are proud to state that all audio content, frequencies, and structural soundscapes within the app are composed and generated using advanced Artificial Intelligence exclusively by Dj Ronny Woods, the creator and founder of the app.

// These AI-crafted frequencies are uniquely tailored for deep relaxation, massage therapy, concentration, and ambient environments in boutique hotels worldwide.

2. How We Use Your Data

The data we collect is utilized strictly to enhance your experience. We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Your data allows us to:

  • Maintain and improve the app's functionality.
  • Ensure your custom 432 Hz playlists are saved and accessible across your devices.
  • Provide customer support and technical assistance to our B2B partners (Hotels and Spas).

3. Data Security

We employ industry-standard encryption and security protocols to protect your information. All connections between your device and our servers are encrypted using modern SSL/TLS standards, ensuring your wellness journey remains private.

4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the updated policy in the app.
